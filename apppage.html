About Lingo

Lingo is a powerful accessibility application designed to break down communication barriers for individuals with hearing impairments, speech difficulties, or anyone who needs assistance with verbal communication. The app provides real-time speech-to-text captions and text-to-speech functionality, making conversations more accessible and inclusive.

Whether you're in a classroom, workplace, or social setting, Lingo helps ensure that everyone can participate in conversations effectively. The app uses advanced speech recognition technology to accurately transcribe spoken words into text, and high-quality text-to-speech to convert written text back into natural-sounding speech.

Key Features

Real-time Speech-to-Text

Accurately transcribes spoken words into text in real-time with high precision.

Text-to-Speech

Converts typed text into natural-sounding speech with customizable voices and accents.

Dark Mode

Reduces eye strain with a comfortable dark mode option for low-light environments.
